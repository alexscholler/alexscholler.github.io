About Me

Hello, I'm Alexandru, the director of eQualsFortyTwo Ltd, a software engineer, and someone who works in building management. Those three roles feed into each other every day.

Working in building management means I see the day-to-day reality of running residential buildings first-hand: maintenance issues, keys, deliveries, inspections, compliance deadlines and the residents behind them. As a software engineer, I turn those real operational problems into tools that actually help. As a director, I make the decisions that take those tools from idea to product, from product design and security to customers and delivery.

That combination is how Handovr came about: a building management platform designed by someone who knows the job, built by someone who writes the code. Along the way I've worked with Python, JavaScript, SQL and modern web technologies, building full-stack applications, data analysis tools and automation workflows.

I'm constantly learning, from code, from running a business, and from the people I work with.
