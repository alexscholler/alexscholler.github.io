Overview

Handovr is a residential building management platform that I designed and built as founder and director of eQualsFortyTwo Ltd. It connects management, concierge teams and residents in one system and follows every issue from the first report to the final sign-off.

It started as a small incident-tracking prototype (my earlier Residential Management System case study) and grew into a commercial, multi-client product used to run day-to-day building operations.

The Problem

Residential buildings are still often run on emails, spreadsheets, paper logbooks and WhatsApp groups. Concierge teams pass information along by word of mouth at shift change, residents can't see what is happening with their issue, and managers have little visibility of compliance deadlines or missed inspections.

  • Slow, untracked responses to maintenance issues
  • Information lost between shifts and teams
  • Compliance certificates tracked in spreadsheets and missed
  • No reliable record of who held which keys, and when

Three Portals, One System

Management

Directors, property and development managers get portfolio oversight: executive dashboard, compliance tracker with expiry reminders, approvals and audit history.

Operations & Concierge

Front-line staff handle handovers, deliveries, keys, defects, inspections, incidents and facility bookings on desktop or mobile.

Residents

Tenants, owners, managing agents and private assistants report issues, approve access, book facilities and servicing slots, and follow building news.

Key Modules

Maintenance

Seven-step workflow: report with photos, occupier approval, management review, contractor assignment, key access, full action log, archive and export.

Inspections

Mobile checklists on daily, weekly, monthly or yearly schedules, with photo evidence, severity ratings and automatic linked defects. Missed rounds are visible.

Servicing & Bookings

Building-wide servicing campaigns (FCU, HIU, fire doors) with resident slot booking, plus facility bookings for pools, gyms, saunas and cinemas.

Keys & Access

Key set register, one-off or recurring collection arrangements, occupier approval, and per-key collection and return logs.

GeoTrack

Staff clock in and out with location and time, hours reporting and CSV export. Privacy-first: location saved only on action, logs auto-deleted after 12 months.

Compliance

Dashboard for Gas Safety, EICR, EPC, Fire Risk Assessments and more, with expiry reminders so nothing lapses.

Deliveries

Barcode registration of parcels with automatic email notifications to residents.

Shift Handover & Incidents

Structured shift handover logs and guided incident reports generated as Word documents.

Community & Records

Announcements and polls, searchable documents hub, unified calendar, contractor and resident directories, Excel exports.

Security & Data Protection

Because Handovr holds residents' personal data across multiple client buildings, security was designed in from the start and built with UK GDPR in mind.

  • Two-factor sign-in (authenticator app or emailed codes) with backup codes and lockout protection
  • Strict data separation between clients and granular role permissions
  • Searchable audit trail of every action
  • Self-service data export and managed erasure with approval
  • Automatic daily backups
